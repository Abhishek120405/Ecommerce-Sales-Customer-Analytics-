# Excel Dashboard Details

The repository's current workbook is `excel/Ecommerce_Sales_Analytics_Dashboard.xlsx`.

## Dashboard worksheet

- 4 dropdown filters: Region, Category, Sales Channel and Month
- 6 KPI cards: Total Orders, Delivered Revenue, Estimated Delivered Profit, Average Order Value, Return Rate and Cancellation Rate
- 6 native Excel charts: Monthly Delivered Revenue (line), Revenue Share by Category (doughnut), Delivered Revenue by Region (bar), Orders by Weekday (column), Top 5 Products by Delivered Revenue (bar), and Order Status Split (doughnut)

## Supporting worksheets

`Insights`, `Sales_Data`, `Calc` and `About` provide supporting findings, the 600-row source table, calculation logic and project notes.

## Data notes

The dataset is synthetic practice data from 2025. Profit is estimated from assumed margins and is not audited company profit. Delivered revenue and delivered profit refer to delivered orders only. Return and cancellation rates use all orders as the denominator.
